**My Favorite Movie: *Mamma Mia!* (2008)**

### Why I Love It
*Mamma Mia!* is pure joy in movie form — a sunny, feel-good musical packed with ABBA hits, gorgeous Greek island views, and non-stop energy. It’s the ultimate comfort watch that always lifts my mood.

> “I have a dream, a song to sing…”  
> — The whole spirit of the movie

#### What Makes It Special
- **Infectious energy** – You can’t help but sing along
- Beautiful location (filmed on the real Greek island of Skopelos)
- Hilarious and heartfelt performances
- A perfect mix of romance, comedy, and mother-daughter bonding

---

### Main Cast
- **Donna Sheridan** – Meryl Streep (Sophie’s free-spirited mother)
- **Sophie Sheridan** – Amanda Seyfried (The bride-to-be)
- **Sam Carmichael** – Pierce Brosnan (One of the possible dads)
- **Bill Anderson** – Stellan Skarsgård (Another possible dad)
- **Harry Bright** – Colin Firth (The third possible dad)
- **Tanya** – Christine Baranski (Donna’s glamorous best friend)
- **Rosie** – Julie Walters (Donna’s fun-loving best friend)

### Fun Facts
- The movie is based on the massively successful stage musical that uses ABBA’s real songs.
- Pierce Brosnan’s singing became an internet meme… and we love him for it.
- There’s a fantastic sequel: ***Mamma Mia! Here We Go Again*** (2018).
- Filming on the Greek islands made the whole cast fall in love with the location.
